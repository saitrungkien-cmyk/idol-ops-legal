Privacy Policy — Idol Ops

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how [Your Company Name] ("we", "us") handles data in Idol Ops ("the App"), an internal tool we use to manage and publish video content to the TikTok accounts we own and operate on behalf of the creators we represent.

The App is for our organization's internal use only. It is not offered to the general public and does not create accounts for external users.

1. Information we process

  • TikTok authorization data: access tokens and account identifiers that our own TikTok accounts grant to the App so it can publish on our behalf.
  • Content: video files, captions, and related metadata that we own or are licensed to use, which we upload or publish through the TikTok Content Posting API.
  • Operational data: internal records of who performed each action (publish, schedule, edit) and when, kept for auditing.

2. How we use it

  • To upload and publish our own video content to our own TikTok accounts.
  • To schedule, organize, and track that content across the accounts we operate.
  • To maintain an internal audit log for accountability.

3. TikTok data

The App uses TikTok's APIs. Our use of information received from TikTok follows the TikTok Developer Terms and applicable TikTok policies. We use TikTok data only to operate the App's publishing features described above.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We do not share the data above with third parties, except service providers that host our infrastructure and only to the extent needed to run the App, and where required by law.

5. Storage and retention

Data is stored on secured infrastructure we control. Access tokens and credentials are encrypted at rest. We retain operational records for as long as needed for our operations and legal obligations, and delete or anonymize data when it is no longer required.

6. Security

We use role-based access control, encryption of secrets, and internal audit logging to protect the data we process.
